# Lessons

- **No em dashes.** Not in site copy (bullets, tickers, labels) and not in replies. Use commas, colons, parentheses, or two sentences. Grep for `—` / `&mdash;` before calling copy done.
- **"Be creative" means show the work, not restyle the card.** A layout swap (flip cards → product grid) wasn't enough; the user wanted something that demonstrates what was built.
- **Creative suggestions must not recycle content already on the page.** Pitching resume metrics (4.2s to 1.1s, $4,740) for the hero read as repetitive because Experience and Projects already show them. Bring a new idea, research real references online, and propose fewer, bolder concepts.
- **Ask before removing a section the user didn't mention.** Proposing to replace the "building / i code" block was unwanted; scope redesigns to exactly the area pointed at.
